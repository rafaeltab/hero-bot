# AI Hero Community Management

## Language

**Discord moderation**:
Reviewing community messages and applying policy to messages or the members who post them. Removing a Discord message is distinct from removing the external material it links to.

**Takedown assistance**:
Helping the course owner pursue removal of externally hosted unauthorized course copies. This is distinct from moderating Discord messages that discuss or link to those copies.

**Pirated course material**:
Course material copied or distributed without the rights holder's permission, regardless of its creator. Material being available for free does not by itself establish piracy.

**Message deletion**:
Removal of a message from a Discord conversation. This is distinct from a member sanction.

**Member sanction**:
A moderation action restricting a member's participation or membership, such as a timeout, kick, or ban.

**Review hold**:
A restriction of a member's ordinary server access pending a moderator decision, presented to members as purgatory. This is distinct from a ban and from deletion of an individual message.

**Restriction level**:
A member's current moderation restriction: unrestricted, purgatory, or banned. It describes the member's state, independently of the action recommended or applied for any individual report.

**Recommended action**:
The moderation action judged appropriate for a case, regardless of whether it is enabled or has been carried out.

**Applied action**:
The moderation action actually carried out for a case. It can differ from the recommended action.

**Moderation evidence**:
The messages, context, and relevant member history supporting a moderation judgment, with their sources and authorship distinguished. Missing material is a limitation of the evidence, not a finding about the member.

**Moderation history**:
Recorded moderation findings and outcomes concerning a member, including corrections and overturned actions. It is distinct from the member's entire message history.

**Moderation judgment**:
An assessment of a message under the moderation policy, consisting of a recommended action, a concise reason including relevant uncertainty, and any offending URLs. It is distinct from authorization to act and from the applied action.

**Moderation finding**:
An assessment of reported conduct made by automated moderation or by a moderator. Its origin is distinct from who carries out an action based on it, and it may subsequently be upheld or overturned.

**Offending URL**:
A URL implicated in a moderation violation. Its inclusion records the policy finding, not proof that the destination hosts pirated material.

**Moderator record**:
The evidence, assessments, actions, outcomes, and review history concerning a reported message, person, or URL. It includes a current summary and a chronological account of what happened.

**Report message**:
The moderator-facing summary of a report, identifying its subject and relevant moderation history. The subject of a report is distinct from its reporter.

**Automatic report message**:
A report message raised by automated moderation, including its recommended and applied actions.

**Confidential report**:
A report submitted privately to moderators, whose contents are not disclosed to other community members. Quoted or supplied misconduct is evidence about its attributed author, not about the reporter.

**Reporter**:
The person submitting a confidential report. This is distinct from the person being reported, if any.

**Moderator correction**:
A moderator's recorded amendment to an assessment or outcome. It preserves the earlier account while identifying what was mistaken and what changed.

**Pending review**:
A report awaiting a moderator's final decision. An automatic action or a moderator-imposed review hold does not complete that review.
